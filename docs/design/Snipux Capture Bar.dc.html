Staying out of the way of a mode that needs the whole screen

The chooser is up when nothing is selected yet, so the entire monitor is under the 62% scrim — no marching ants, no frame, nothing to point at. Which means every pixel it occupies is a pixel Window mode can't preview and Freeform can't trace. Three answers, and they differ on what happens the instant you arm a mode: 3a shrinks it to a status pill in the spot the floating bar will take over; 3b parks it against the monitor's top edge as a hover tab; 3c deletes it and moves the state onto the cursor. Use the Choosing / Armed switch on each to see both halves — the second state is the actual design work.

The pre-capture bar

What shipped puts three different kinds of thing in one undifferentiated row: what to capture (Region / Window / Full screen / Freeform), where it goes afterwards (Review / Copy / Save), and no way to actually commit. Region and Copy both look "selected" in the same style, but they're answers to different questions — so the bar reads as seven peers where two of them happen to be lit. All three options below separate those axes; they differ in how much of the after-decision belongs here at all. 2a keeps it as one value, 2b spells it out as a sentence, 2c removes it.
