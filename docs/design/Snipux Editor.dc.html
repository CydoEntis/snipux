snipux · capture & annotate

Two takes on the toolbar

Both fix the same three things: the screenshot now sits on a workspace with a real edge and shadow so you can see what you are editing, the 13 tools are grouped into icon clusters instead of a single text row, and tool settings are pulled out of the bar. 1a puts settings in a contextual second row; 1b puts them in a side inspector with a live annotation list. Every tool draws for real — pick one and drag on the canvas.
